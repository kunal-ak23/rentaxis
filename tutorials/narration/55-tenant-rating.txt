Tenant rating scores every tenant from zero to one hundred, from how they have actually paid and behaved over the last two years. It is a premium feature. Once it is switched on for your organisation, Tenant ratings appears under Leasing. Grade A, excellent, is eighty-five and above. B, good, is seventy and above. C, fair, is fifty and above, and D, poor, is below fifty. Tenants without enough history yet are marked New. Filter by grade or by property, search by name, sort by score, and export the list as a spreadsheet. Select a grade to see why. Every tenant starts at seventy. Each event that moved the score is listed with its date and its points. Fatima's cheques that cleared on time add one point each. Her cheque that bounced in April takes away fifteen. That leaves sixty, a C. Late payments, penalties, write-offs and missed meetings take points away too. Early renewals add them, and events more than a year old count at half weight. Rajesh has scored eighty-one, a B. A Company Admin can add a manual adjustment of up to thirty points either way, with a reason. Plus five: referred a new tenant. Rajesh moves to eighty-six, an A, and the history shows who added it. The same grade appears in Renewal season, on each contract, and on the tenant's profile, so it is in front of you when you decide a renewal offer. Ratings are recalculated every night, and the details panel is always up to date. Tenants never see their own rating.
