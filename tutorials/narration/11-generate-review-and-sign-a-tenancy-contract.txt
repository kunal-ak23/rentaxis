This tutorial reviews a prepared contract-ready lease without mutating the tutorial tenant. Open the RentAxis Tutorial Studio lease for Sara Mansour and confirm the renter, property, unit, dates, rent, deposit, and payment-plan summary before reviewing the document. Contract generation should never be used to discover basic lease errors. Open the Contract tab to see the authenticated Preview Contract control and the settlement link. Select Preview Contract when you are ready to inspect the agreement; if the browser does not render PDF inline, use the provided Open in new tab fallback. Check the tenant and renter names, unit, term, financial amounts, installment schedule, and signature areas, while keeping document URLs and credentials out of recordings. The prepared lease is currently Pending Signature, which means the reviewed agreement is waiting for the authorised renter’s decision. Pending Signature is intentionally different from Active: it does not yet prove that the agreement was accepted or that the unit is occupied. Use the renter-facing portal tutorial to demonstrate separate review, rejection, and acceptance actions with an approved account. Finish by leaving this administrator view on the current contract workspace, ready for the authorised signature workflow.
