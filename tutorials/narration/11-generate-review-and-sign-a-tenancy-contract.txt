This tutorial generates a tenancy contract from a draft, and has the tenant review and accept it in the tenant portal. As a Company Admin, open the draft for unit A-201, Rajesh Kumar's contract. Its status is Draft. Open the Attachments tab and select Preview Contract. The contract is rendered with the organisation's details, the tenant, the term, every charge, and the cheques, and nothing is saved yet. If anything is wrong, cancel and correct the draft first. When it is right, select Confirm and Save. The contract is generated and numbered, the status becomes Pending Signature, and the contract is listed under Contract documents. Now sign in as the tenant. Rajesh's portal shows the new contract for A-201, awaiting his signature. He can download it to read, and then accept or reject it. Select Accept Tenancy Contract, and confirm. The portal now says it was accepted, and is awaiting the landlord. Had he rejected it, the contract would go back to Draft, to be corrected and generated again. Back as the Company Admin, the contract shows Accepted by tenant, with the date. It stays pending until it is posted, which has its own tutorial.
