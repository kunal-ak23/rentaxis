This tutorial shows a renter how to read the payment schedule, payment history, and receipt controls. Sign in with the prepared Ahmed Hassan account and open My Payments.

The page starts with the next cheque due, its amount, due date, cheque number, property, and unit. The active lease card separates the rent amount from the total payable amount. Use those labels to confirm that the installment belongs to the right home before following up with the property team.

Payment History lists completed installments with their amounts and dates. A green completed state is different from pending, deposited, cleared, failed, or overdue. Receipt buttons are available on completed rows. A receipt should identify the organisation, renter, lease, amount, date, and reference; download or share it only through an approved channel because it can contain personal and financial information. If a future or historical amount looks wrong, send the installment reference to the property team instead of submitting a duplicate payment.

Return to the renter dashboard to confirm the next payment, the active unit, lease dates, rent, and the Facilities and Parking entry point. Online checkout is not advertised in this fixture because the organisation’s gateway is inactive. When an approved gateway is active and an installment is eligible, RentAxis can expose an online-payment action. Complete a live provider flow only once and wait for the callback; repeated clicks can create duplicate or confusing attempts. The schedule explains what is due, while history and receipts prove what has completed.
