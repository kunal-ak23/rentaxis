This tutorial shows a Tenant's own view of rent payments in Oasis Crest Properties. Sign in as the prepared Tenant and open My Payments. Each row shows its amount, due date, and cheque number, and an overdue badge appears once a due row has slipped past its date. A row waiting to be collected is not the same as one already deposited at the bank, one that has cleared, or one that has bounced — each state is labelled plainly so the Tenant always knows where their own money stands. Scroll to Payment History for the same schedule read the other way: every past cheque with its final state and, wherever it has actually been collected, a receipt to open. Open one receipt and confirm it identifies the organisation, the Tenant, the tenancy contract, the amount, the date, and a reference number. Return to the Tenant's dashboard. Pay Now only appears when this organisation and this specific property both have an active, tested payment gateway behind them — configured on the admin side in Settings, under Payments — so its absence here is not a fault, it is simply not switched on for this property yet. A Tenant should be able to tell, from this page alone, exactly what is due, what has already moved, and what proof exists for anything already paid.
