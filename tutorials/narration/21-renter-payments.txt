This tutorial shows a Tenant's own view of rent in Oasis Crest Properties, signed in as Ahmed Hassan. The home page shows the next payment: 21,250, due on the first of October and still within the grace period. Select View all payments. My Payments opens with what is due now. The amount due is 21,250: cheque 5, number 200104, dated the first of October. The landlord already holds this cheque and banks it on its date, so keep the funds in your account. Its row shows the due date, the amount payable and the running total of everything due. Payment History reads the other way. Cheques 1, 2 and 3 have cleared, and each has a receipt voucher to download. Cheque 4 is at the bank, awaiting clearance, so there is nothing to pay. Select Receipt Voucher on any cleared cheque to download its receipt. From these two pages a Tenant can see what is due, what has already moved, and the proof of what has been paid.
