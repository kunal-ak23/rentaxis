This tutorial follows a tenancy contract that ended early, from termination to the deposit refund. As the Accountant, open Grace Okafor's contract for R-203: 70,000 of rent a year from 15 October 2025. She relocated and left on 31 May 2026. The contract is Closed, and its settlement summary shows the 3,500 deposit held, 750 of deductions and a refund of 11,332.19. Open the Cheques tab. Three rent cheques cleared. The July cheque was dated after she left, so termination returned it to her. In the Rent Receivable ledger below, the reversal of its post-dated cheque journal debits Rent Receivable with 17,500. Termination also wrote a TCR journal: the rent after 31 May was never earned, so Advance Rent is debited and Rent Receivable credited with 26,082.19. The balance ends at 8,582.19 in credit, because Grace paid more than she used. Now open the settlement. The statement is computed from the ledger: earned rent 43,917.81, received 56,000 with the deposit, a receivable balance of minus 8,582.19, and 3,500 of deposit held. Move-out cleaning of 750 is deducted, so the refund to Grace is 11,332.19, and it has been paid, with nothing outstanding. Open the settlement journal, STL. It debits Security Deposit with 3,500 and Rent Receivable with 8,582.19, and credits Maintenance Charges with 750 and Refunds payable with 11,332.19. Until it is paid, the refund is a liability. It was paid by transfer five days later. In Journal Voucher, the payment voucher BPV debits Refunds payable and credits the Emirates Islamic account for the Residences with 11,332.19, and the liability is cleared.
