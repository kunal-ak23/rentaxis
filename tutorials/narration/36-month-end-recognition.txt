Rent arrives in a few cheques but is earned every day. This tutorial closes September. As the Accountant, open Omar Haddad's contract for R-101 and its recognition schedule. The rent is 72,000 for 365 days, so the day rate is 72,000 divided by 365. Each month is that day rate times its real number of days: October's 31 days are 6,115.07, and February's 28 days are 5,523.29. September is the last month, so it absorbs the rounding, 5,917.79, and the schedule adds up to exactly 72,000. Every month up to August is Posted, with its journal. September is still Planned. Now open Month-end recognition. Enter 30 September, the last day of the month you are closing, and select Preview. Nothing is written yet. Nine September periods are waiting, grouped by building, 52,359.10 in all. R-202 has two rows: the rent, and a parking fee that is also earned over the term. Read the total before you post it. Select Run recognition and confirm. Each planned period becomes its own journal, dated the last day of its month. Nine journals are posted, each a CIL dated 30 September. Open the General Ledger for September, on Advance Rent and Rental Income for the Residences. Each CIL debits Advance Rent and credits Rental Income. Advance Rent falls by 35,112.32, and Rental Income rises by exactly the same amount. Back on Omar's contract, September now shows Posted, with its journal. Recognition also runs on its own every night, so closing a month is mostly a check that nothing is waiting.
