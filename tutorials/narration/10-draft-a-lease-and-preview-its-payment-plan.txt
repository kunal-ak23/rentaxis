This tutorial previews the lease workflow and reviews a prepared payment plan before any unit is occupied. Open Leases and select Draft Lease to see the five-stage wizard without saving a new record. In Parties, confirm that the property, prepared vacant unit, and renter are selected deliberately rather than accepting the first search result. Continue through Terms and review the start and end dates, rent, deposit, payment method, and any reference fields available at the draft stage. In Charges, review tax treatment and keep recurring or one-time charges explicit so the renter and finance team can understand them later. Continue to Payment Plan and inspect how installment count, due dates, and amounts are represented. Check the first and final installment and confirm that the schedule reconciles with the displayed lease terms. If a final installment carries a rounding difference, verify the overall total. Leave the wizard unsaved so the tutorial tenant remains deterministic. Next, open the prepared pending-signature lease and review its renter, unit, dates, rent, deposit, and current status. Its payment schedule is editable while it is pending signature; inspect the rows without entering cheque details or saving changes. A pending-signature lease is not proof of a signed agreement. Finish on the schedule preview, ready for the contract and signature workflow covered in the next tutorial.
