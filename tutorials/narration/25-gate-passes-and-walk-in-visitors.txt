Open Gate Pass and start with Gate Pass Report. Set the from and to dates, then use the property selector to scope the audit. The table records scan time, direction, result, property, unit, guest, phone, vehicle, purpose, pass type, reason, and the operator who scanned it.

Use the property filter to narrow the report to one building and confirm the scan count and empty-state guidance. Keep the chosen date range and property scope visible whenever you export or share an audit view.

When investigating a period, clear or widen the filters and use Export CSV when records are present. This report is the audit trail for gate decisions; validate the current pass at the gate and record both entry and exit accurately.
