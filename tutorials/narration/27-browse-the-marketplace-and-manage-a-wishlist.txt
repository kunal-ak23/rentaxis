This tutorial uses the Tenant-facing marketplace. Sign in with the prepared Tenant account and open the Oasis Crest Properties marketplace. Browse the published listings and apply the bedrooms, rent range, furnishing, and availability filters. Confirm that the result count and cards update to the selected scope. Open a listing and review its images, price, availability, property and unit details, amenities, description, and contact action. Select Add to Wishlist. The same action both saves the listing privately and, if a note was entered, sends that note to the property team as an enquiry — it is not two separate steps. Open Wishlist and confirm the listing appears; a wishlist is a private shortlist and does not reserve the unit. Remove it once with Remove from Wishlist and confirm the list updates, then add it again for the next step. Switch to the manager session and open that listing's View Enquiries. The Tenant's enquiry and note should appear there for follow-up. Return to the Tenant account: removing a wishlist item does not withdraw an enquiry already sent, so treat saving and contacting as related but separate actions. Finish by clearing the filters and confirming the catalogue returns to its broader view.
