Sign in with the prepared renter account and open the Tutorial Studio marketplace. Published cards expose approved property information while keeping tenant administration private. Review the catalogue and result count before narrowing it.

Use the marketplace search and filters to narrow inventory by the available property, rent, bedroom, and availability controls. Check that the selected scope is reflected in the cards and that clearing filters restores the broader catalogue.

The Wishlist route is a separate saved-property surface for the authenticated renter. Confirm the access boundary rather than treating a saved property as a reservation or an approval queue.

Use the registration page when a visitor needs an account before saving or expressing interest. Capture only the fields the form requests and keep account credentials private.

Finish on Privacy and legal information. Review the published guidance for data use, terms, and deletion before a visitor submits personal information.
