Bank reconciliation proves that the bank account in Miftah agrees with the bank's own statement. Open Accounting and, under Bank, choose Bank reconciliation. Each bank account is listed with its ledger account, and the bank's TRN is already on file for Palm Ridge Residences. Choose Import statement and pick the August 2026 statement file. Miftah recognises the columns: date, description, reference, cheque number, debit, credit and balance. Save the mapping, and next month's file imports in one step. The preview reads the opening balance, 585,587.81, and the closing balance, 622,895.31, from the file, and finds six new lines. Import them. Now open the workspace: the statement on the left, the books on the right. Select Auto-match. The three cheque clearings find their cheques by cheque number, with high confidence. Confirm all high. Three lines are left, because only the bank knew about them. The first is the account maintenance charge of 105. Record it from the line as a bank charge, and tick that the amount includes VAT. Because the bank's TRN is on file, the journal debits the charge with 100 and Input VAT with 5, and credits the bank with 105. Book it. The profit credit of 412.50 is booked as interest: debit the bank, credit Bank interest. The inward transfer of 2,500 has not been identified yet. Book it as an unidentified receipt. The bank is debited and Unidentified bank receipts is credited, until the tenant is found and the money is received against their instalment. Now reconcile August. Enter the period, the first to the thirty-first of August, and start the first reconciliation. The statement and the books both read 622,895.31, nothing is in transit, and the difference is zero. Every check passes, so select Finalize. Finalising locks this bank account through 31 August, so no entry can change a month you have signed off. The reconciliation report is ready as a PDF, in English or Arabic.
