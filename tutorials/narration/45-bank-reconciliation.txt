Bank reconciliation proves that the bank account in Miftah agrees with the bank's own statement. Open Accounting and, under Bank, choose Bank reconciliation, then the Emirates Islamic account for Palm Ridge Residences. Select Import and choose the August 2026 statement file. The first time, map the file's columns to date, description, cheque number, debit, credit and balance, and save the mapping for next month. Miftah reads the opening and closing balances from the file and checks the running balance line by line. Select Auto-match. Cheque clearings match by cheque number and supplier payments by their reference. Review the high-confidence matches and confirm them. A few lines are left, because only the bank knew about them. The first is the account maintenance charge of 105, which includes VAT. Choose Book from line, then Bank charge. The journal debits Bank Charges with 100 and Input VAT with 5, and credits the bank with 105. The second is a profit credit of 412.50, which is booked the same way as interest income. The third is an inward transfer of 2,500 that nobody has identified. Choose Suspense. The journal debits the bank and credits Unidentified bank receipts. The money is in the bank before anyone knows whose it is, and when the tenant is found you receive it against their instalment from the same line. Now select Reconcile for August. Miftah sets the statement's closing balance against the book balance, then lists the deposits in transit, which are cheques you banked on the last two days of the month that the bank will credit in September, and any payments not yet presented. The difference reads zero. Select Finalise. Finalising locks this bank account through 31 August, so no entry can change a month you have signed off.
