This tutorial covers facilities and parking in Oasis Crest Properties: what can be booked, how a Tenant asks for it, and how a manager approves. Signed in as the Company Admin, open the Oasis Crest Residence Tower property. The Amenities tab lists the Residents Fitness Centre: bookable, active, and open to every tower. The Parking tab lists spot B2-18 on level B2, available. Now sign in as the Tenant Ahmed Hassan. From the home page, open Facilities and Parking. Select Request on the fitness centre, pick a preferred date and add a note with the time, then Submit Request. The card now shows one pending request; nothing is booked until a manager approves. Request parking spot B2-18 the same way, with a note about the second car. Both are listed under My Requests as Pending, and either can still be withdrawn with Cancel Request. Back as the Company Admin, open Operations, Bookings, where both requests are waiting. Open the fitness centre request. The drawer shows the Tenant, the unit, the preferred date and the note, and the other requests for the same facility, so a clash is visible before deciding. Add a note to the Tenant and select Approve. Then open the parking request and approve it too; an approved spot can be released from here later. Back as the Tenant, My Requests shows both as Approved, with the manager's notes. When the second car goes, Release Spot hands the parking spot back, so another Tenant can request it.
