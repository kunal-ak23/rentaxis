This tutorial ends a tenancy contract before its term is over: terminate it, settle the deposit, and pay the refund. Ahmed Hassan's contract for unit A-101 runs to the end of December, but he moved out on the thirty-first of August. First, his July cheque. It is already at the bank, and the bank has confirmed it, so find it in the cheque register and choose Clear. Open his contract, and under More actions, select Terminate. Set the termination date to the thirty-first of August. Rent earned to that day is 56,589.04, and the 28,410.96 after it is reversed, because it was never earned. The October cheque is dated after he left, so it is returned to him. Terminate, and confirm. The settlement opens, computed from the ledger. Ahmed's deposit of 22,000 is held, and the balance shows the landlord owes him 7,160.96. Deduct 750 for cleaning after move-out, and the refund to Ahmed is 28,410.96. Save the draft, then Finalize. Finalizing writes the settlement journal, and the refund is owed to him until it is paid. Choose Pay refund. A payment voucher opens, filled in from the settlement: Ahmed, the refunds payable account, and the amount. Pay from the building's bank account, the Emirates Islamic account for Residence Tower, by bank transfer, enter the transfer reference, and Post. Back on Ahmed's settlement, the refund of 28,410.96 is paid in full, and nothing is outstanding.
