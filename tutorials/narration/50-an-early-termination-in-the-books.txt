This tutorial follows the early termination from tutorial 48 into the books. Open Ahmed Hassan's contract for A-101: with its settlement finalized, it is Closed. On the Cheques tab, open Journal Vouchers. Terminating on the thirty-first of August wrote three things. The October cheque's PDR is reversed, so its 21,250 is back on Rent Receivable. August's recognition is posted again, so the last month absorbs the rounding. And TCR-26/2 takes off the unearned rent. Open it. TCR-26/2 debits Advance Rent and credits Rent Receivable with 28,410.96: the rent from September to December, which was never earned. Finalizing the settlement wrote STL-26/2. The deposit of 22,000 is released from Security Deposit. Rent Receivable is debited 7,160.96, clearing what the landlord owed Ahmed on account. The cleaning deduction of 750 is credited to Maintenance Charges. And the 28,410.96 left is owed to Ahmed, in Refunds payable. Paying the refund wrote BPV-26/2. Refunds payable is debited, and the building's bank account is credited 28,410.96. Refunds payable is kept at head office, so two inter-property clearing lines balance head office against the building. Below the list, the contract account by account. Advance Rent is at zero. The bank keeps 58,839.04. PDC Receivable and Rent Receivable are at zero, and Rental Income shows the 56,589.04 earned. Security Deposit and Refunds payable are at zero too. What the bank keeps is what was earned: the rent, the admin fee of 1,500, and the 750 for cleaning. Revenue recognition stops at the termination date: September to December are cancelled, and the schedule totals 56,589.04, the rent earned to the thirty-first of August.
