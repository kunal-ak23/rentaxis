This tutorial reviews day-to-day administration for a prepared active lease. Open the RentAxis Tutorial Studio lease for Ahmed Hassan and confirm its active status, renter, unit, dates, rent, deposit, and payment summary. Open Payment schedule to compare the installment dates, amounts, methods, cheque metadata, and current statuses; treat cleared and deposited rows as historical records and avoid changing them without an approved correction process. Open Documents to review the attachment area and the document-name field. Upload only a lease-owned synthetic file through an approved workflow, never a real identity document or unrelated credential. Open Interactions and wait for the existing activity list to load. Select Log interaction to preview the structured form for channel, direction, time, summary, outcome, and follow-up date; leave it unsaved so the tutorial tenant remains deterministic. Interaction notes should be factual and must not contain passwords, payment credentials, or irrelevant personal information. The active lease view keeps the operational record, schedule, documents, and communication history together for the next operator. Finish by closing the form and leaving the lease on its current active state.
