This tutorial covers the end of a tenancy contract: renewing it, extending it, or ending it and settling the deposit. As a Company Admin, open Leasing, then Tenancy Contracts. Start with Ahmed Hassan's contract for unit A-101, which ends on the thirty-first of December. Select Renew. The next term, all of next year, is filled in, and the charge lines are copied across. Choose By percent and enter 5, and the rent goes from 85,000 to 89,250. The deposit is carried forward, so it is not collected again. Select Renew. A new draft opens for next year, linked to the contract it renews. It is signed and posted like any other draft. An extension is for a shorter stay on the same contract. Open Sara Mansour's contract for M-1501, and under More actions, select Extend Contract. Set the new end date to the thirty-first of January. Add a Rent line of 9,500 for the extra month, and the cheque that pays for it. Select Extend Contract. The end date moves, and the extension posts its own entries. Ending a contract starts with a notice. Open Rajesh Kumar's contract for A-103, and under More actions, select Give notice. Rajesh gave notice on the thirty-first of July, to move out on the thirty-first of August. Select Give notice, and the contract is marked Notice Given. It keeps collecting until it ends. Now select Terminate, and set the termination date to the thirty-first of August. The figures show the rent earned to that date, and the unearned rent that will be reversed. Cheques dated up to the move-out are kept for collection, and later ones go back to the tenant. Add a note, select Terminate, and confirm. The contract is terminated, and the settlement opens. It shows the deposit held, and a receivable balance below zero, because the landlord owes Rajesh a little. Add a deduction for cleaning, of 1,500, and the refund drops to 11,612.33. Save draft keeps it for someone to review. Three kept cheques are still being collected, so tick the confirmation, then select Finalize, and confirm. The settlement is finalized with its own journal, and the refund is ready to pay.
