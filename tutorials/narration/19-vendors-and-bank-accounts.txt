This tutorial reviews the vendor directory, finance transactions, and property bank-account register. Use an authorised tenant administrator and synthetic data only.

Open Vendors first. The directory keeps supplier identity separate from the transactions that later reference it. Review the vendor name, tax-registration column, contact person, phone, payable-account field, active status, and the available pay or edit actions. Add Vendor is available when a new supplier has passed the organisation’s approval process. Use the legal name, service category, contact details, and tax information from an approved source; never enter a real person’s private information in a tutorial. Keep a vendor active only while new operational work may use it, and deactivate it when policy requires history to remain but new use must stop.

The Transactions workspace is where vendor-linked expenses appear alongside rental income and cheque events. Split rows identify costs allocated across units or properties, while organisation rows represent whole-portfolio expenses. Use the property, account, and date filters before reconciling a supplier invoice. A vendor payment should reference the approved invoice and should not duplicate an automated rent, penalty, or settlement posting.

Open Bank Accounts to review the approved receiving accounts for the tenant. The prepared Tutorial Studio Bank entry shows a synthetic account number, IBAN, AED currency, Dubai Main branch, the linked Residence Tower property, a Default Account badge, and Active status. Choose Default only when finance has approved that account as the primary destination for the relevant property. Account identifiers can be sensitive; use masked or synthetic values in recordings. Editing should preserve the audit trail, and deactivation is safer than deletion when history still references the account.
