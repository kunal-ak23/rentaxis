Every supplier bill and every bank movement in Miftah needs two kinds of master data: vendors and bank accounts. As the Accountant, open Vendors. Each supplier has its tax registration number, a contact, and its own payable account in the ledger, with what is open, overdue or paid in advance. Now add a new supplier. Select Add Vendor and enter the legal name, Gulf Shield Pest Control. Enter the fifteen-digit TRN: without it, the input VAT on this supplier's bills cannot be claimed. Set the payment terms to 45 days, so every bill gets its due date automatically, then the contact details. You do not choose a payable account: Miftah creates one for the vendor and keeps it. Save, and Gulf Shield appears with its own payable account. Open BlueWave Cleaning to see a vendor at work. The header shows its TRN and terms. Open items lists every bill with its voucher, due date, amount, paid and open. The August bill, dated 25 August, falls due on 24 September. The statement of account is a PDF for any dates, in English or Arabic. Bank accounts are set up by the Company Admin. Palm Ridge has two Emirates Islamic accounts, one for each building. The account details stay hidden in this video. Each bank account is linked to its property and to that building's own bank account in the chart of accounts, so a cleared cheque or a supplier payment lands in the right building's books. One account is the default. Open one to edit it: the linked property, the chart of accounts link, and the default flag are all here. With vendors and bank accounts in place, every voucher has somewhere to post.
