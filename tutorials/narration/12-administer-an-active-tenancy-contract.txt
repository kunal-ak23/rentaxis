This tutorial covers the everyday work on a tenancy contract once it is active. As a Company Admin, open the contract for unit M-1501, posted in the previous tutorial. Its status is Active. Record payment and Renew sit at the top, and More actions holds the rest: extend, amend, add a charge, transfer, give notice, terminate, and the tenant's ledger. A posted contract is never edited directly; each change is its own entry. Sara is taking a second parking bay from the first of October, so select Add charge. Set the effective date, enter the Ejari number of the addendum, and give a reason. Add a Parking Fee line of 750, which is earned over the rest of the term. Then enter the cheque that pays for it, number 500406, for the same amount, and the cheques match the addendum's value. Select Add charge. The contract value rises to 140,750, and there are now six cheques. Open the Attachments tab. Under Addenda and Ejari, the addendum is listed with its own journal and its Ejari number. Supporting documents keep the paperwork with the contract. Type a name, Move-in inspection report, and attach the file. It is listed with buttons to download or delete it. Open the Activities tab and select Log interaction. Choose Call and Outbound, and write a short summary. Set the outcome to Positive, and save. The note records who logged it and when, so whoever picks up the contract next can see what was agreed. Keep notes factual, and never put passwords or card details in them.
