This tutorial sets up the books for an organisation moving to Miftah from another system. Signed in as the Company Admin, open Accounting and choose Year End Closing. Set the books start date, here 1 September 2025, and the month your fiscal year starts. Miftah locks the day before, so nothing can be posted into the period your old system still owns. From here on the accountant takes over. Open Import Batches under One-time setup. Contracts that are still running come across from the cut-over workbook, with the cheques that are still outstanding. Posting a batch writes each contract's journals on its own dates and catches up its monthly income, and a batch can be reversed as a whole while you check it. The accounts these contracts create, such as rent receivable, post-dated cheques, advance rent and deposits, are worked out from the contracts. You never type them. Now open Opening Balances. The derived rows are greyed out. Enter everything else as it stood on the last day before the books start. For Palm Ridge that is 250,000 in the Residences bank account, 180,000 in the Business Centre account, 12,600 owed to Coastline Facility Services, and the capital that balances the rest, 417,400. Check that the difference is zero and select Post. Miftah writes one opening balance journal dated 31 August 2025. Opening balances are the last step. Once they are posted, an import batch cannot be posted or reversed until you reverse the opening balances first. The 12,600 owed to Coastline is one real invoice, so open AP opening items and record it with its number and due date. It now appears in payables aging and can be paid in a payment run, and the supplier's balance and the open invoice agree. Finally open Cut-over reconciliation. For every account it shows the balance in Miftah, the figure from your old system's trial balance, and the difference. When the difference column reads zero, the books have moved across intact.
