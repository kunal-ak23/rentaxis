This tutorial follows rent cheques through Cheque and Cash Collection, as Maya Khoury, a property manager at Oasis Crest Properties. Due lists every cheque whose date has arrived and that has not cleared yet, across every property. Overdue narrows it to cheques past their grace period, such as Fatima Al Zaabi's May cheque, still waiting to be banked. Before a cheque goes to the bank, attach its photo. Choose Scan cheques, find the contract, here Ahmed Hassan's, and continue. Pick the photo of his October cheque and extract; reading it takes a few seconds. Miftah reads the cheque number, the bank, the payer, the date and the amount, and matches it to cheque 5 on his contract, dated the first of October. Check the row and approve: the scan is attached. To deposit is where banking starts. Tick the cheques going to the bank today: Ahmed Hassan's 200104 and Fatima's 300204, both dated the first of October. The selected total, 36,750, should match the deposit slip. Deposit Batch records both as deposited today, each into its own property's bank account. When the bank confirms a cheque has cleared, find it in the Cheque register. Ahmed's July cheque, 200103, is still deposited. Choose Clear, check the account it cleared into, and confirm. It is now Cleared, with its receipt number beside it. Not every cheque clears. Fatima's 300204 comes back unpaid. Choose Bounce, pick the reason the bank gave, here Payment stopped, and confirm. Miftah records the return, proposes the contract's cheque-return charge of 1,000 for approval, and asks what happens next. Choose Replace now. Fatima hands over a new cheque for the full 15,500. Enter its number, 300295, its date, the fifteenth of October, and its bank. The replacements cover the whole amount, so nothing is left owing. Replace. The Post-dated view lists the cheques maturing in the next two weeks with a running total, and Fatima's new cheque is already there. The same story is on each tenancy contract. On Ahmed's Cheques tab, 200103 is now Cleared, and 200104 is Deposited, with the bank read from its scan. Nothing is ever deleted: every cheque stays traceable, from registered to deposited, cleared, returned or replaced.
