This tutorial follows a rent cheque through Cheque and Cash Collection in Oasis Crest Properties. Open the Due pill first. It lists every registered cheque coming up for collection, and the Overdue view beside it flags anything already past its date, across every property, not just one contract. Move to the Deposit pill. Select one or more cheques that have been collected and bank them together — the register moves them from registered to deposited in one batch, and a note can travel with the batch for anything the deposit slip does not already say. Open the full Cheque register for a wider view: every cheque on every contract, filterable by status, mode and property, with the totals for the current filter shown at the top. If a deposited cheque comes back unpaid, the Return and replace pill is where that is recorded, with the actual failure reason, before new cheques are cut for the same balance — the original bounce stays visible, it is never deleted. The Post-dated pill groups every upcoming post-dated cheque by month with a running total, useful for planning ahead rather than acting on any one row. Finally, open Ahmed Hassan's tenancy contract and its own Payments tab. Alongside the same register actions, this is where scanned cheque images are uploaded in bulk for every registered post-dated cheque still waiting on paper, instead of attaching them one at a time. Whichever pill is open, the same cheque and the same contract stay traceable end to end, from registered to deposited, cleared, bounced or replaced.
