This tutorial reviews the rent-cheque workspace without changing payment records in the fresh tutorial tenant. Open Payment Schedule and review the cleared, collected, overdue, and total-due summaries, then inspect the property, unit, renter, amount, due date, cheque number, method, status, and available action for representative rows. Pending rows are ready for an authorised operator to collect; collected rows can move to deposit, and deposited rows can be cleared or marked failed only after the bank result and the correct failure reason are confirmed. Open the prepared Ahmed Hassan lease and compare its payment schedule with the finance workspace, including cheque metadata, due dates, cleared history, deposited state, and the remaining pending installment. Do not enter cheque details or select Collect, Deposit, Clear, Mark Failed, or Bulk Attach in this rehearsal. Each real transition should retain its operator, timestamp, amount, reference, receipt, and any related penalty so finance can reconstruct the lifecycle. Finish on the finance table with its filters and distinct operational states visible.
