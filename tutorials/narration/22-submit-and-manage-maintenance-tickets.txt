This tutorial follows a maintenance ticket in Oasis Crest Properties, from the Tenant's report to closure, with the work assigned to the maintenance supervisor. Signed in as the Tenant Ahmed Hassan, open My Tickets and select Create Ticket. Give it a short title and describe the problem. The property and unit come from the Tenant's contract. Choose the category, HVAC, and the priority, High, then select Create Ticket. The ticket joins the list as Open. Now sign in as the Company Admin. Khalid Rahman is on the staff list as the Maintenance Supervisor, but tickets are assigned to people who can sign in. In Settings, Users and staff, select New User. Enter his name and email, choose Company User, the role for the maintenance team, and select Provision User. No password is set here: Khalid gets an email invite to choose his own. His login now appears in the Users list as a Company User, with Resend invite in case the email is lost. Now open the new ticket from Operations, Tickets. Select Assign To and pick Khalid Rahman. The status moves to Assigned, and Khalid can update the tickets assigned to him. Reply to the Tenant with the visit time, and set the estimated hours, so the Tenant knows when to expect him. When the work starts, select Start Work. When it is finished, select Mark Resolved. Back as the Tenant, the resolved ticket shows a six-digit closure code. The Tenant shares it only when the work is done. The Company Admin enters the code under Close with OTP and selects Close. The ticket is now Closed. Its activity history records the assignment, each status change and the closure with the code, with who did it and when. Ticket Reports counts tickets by status, category and priority, with the average resolution time and the average satisfaction. Finally, back as the Tenant, rate the service: five stars and a short comment, then Submit Rating. The rating is saved with the ticket and counts towards the satisfaction average.
