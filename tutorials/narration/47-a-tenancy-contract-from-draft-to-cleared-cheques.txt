This tutorial follows one tenancy contract from the first draft to its cheques clearing at the bank. As a Company Admin, open Leasing, then Tenancy Contracts, and select Draft Tenancy Contract. Only vacant units are offered: choose A-202, then the tenant, Rajesh Kumar. On Terms, the contract starts on the first of November, and the end date fills in a year later. Enter 42,000 as the rent for the full term, and set the number of cheques to four. On Charges, the rent line is already there, and its Ledger has filled itself in. Add a Security Deposit of 4,000 and an Admin Fee of 1,000. Each Ledger fills in the moment the charge is chosen, and the contract value is 47,000. Save the draft, and generate the cheques. Separate cheque for one-time charges is ticked, so the four rent cheques of 10,500 come with a cheque of their own for the deposit, and one for the admin fee, both dated today. To put two on one cheque, tick them and choose Merge. Generate Cheque Numbers fills every row, counting up from 600101. The review runs every check a post would. It is ready, so select Post Contract, and the contract is Active. On its Cheques tab, all six cheques are registered: the rent cheques wait for their dates, and the deposit and fee cheques are due today. Open Cheque and Cash Collection, then To deposit, which lists every cheque whose date has arrived. Today's deposit and fee cheques are at the end: tick both, and choose Deposit Batch. When the bank confirms, find the deposit cheque in the register, and choose Clear. The dialog shows the bank account it clears into. Confirm, and it is Cleared, with its receipt number. The fee cheque clears the same way.
