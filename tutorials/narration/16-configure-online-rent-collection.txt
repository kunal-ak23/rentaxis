This tutorial configures online rent collection in Oasis Crest Properties. Open Settings, then Payments. Select the approved provider and confirm Test Mode is on before entering any credentials. Enter the synthetic API key, API secret, and webhook secret — once saved, secrets stay masked, so use Test Connection to confirm the setup without ever showing a real credential on camera. Save the configuration. Still in Payments, find the online-payment switch, choose a property, and turn it on only once the gateway is configured and tested. Switch to Settings, then Rent, to show that the due day of month, grace days, and the per-day late rate live in their own section, separate from the payment gateway itself. Finally, switch to the Tenant's own account and open My Payments. Once a property has online payment switched on and a cheque there is due, Pay Now appears next to it; on any other property, or before the switch is on, it does not. Getting collection online right is two settings working together: a tested gateway, and a property that has actually been switched on to use it.
