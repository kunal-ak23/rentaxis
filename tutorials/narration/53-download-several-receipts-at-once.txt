A tenant asks for every receipt of the year so far. Open the tenancy contract, then its Cheques tab. In the cheque grid, every cleared cheque has a tick box. Tick the rent for January, February and March. The button counts what you ticked: Combined receipt, three. Select it, and one PDF downloads, with one receipt on each page: the amount received, the cheque number, and the date it cleared. For a whole year, use Select year. Pick twenty twenty-six, and every payment that cleared that year is ticked at once: eleven of them, the security deposit included. Download, and the year's receipts arrive as a single file. Only cleared cheques can be ticked. A cheque that has not been banked yet has no receipt, so it has no tick box. One download covers one contract, up to a hundred receipts. Tenants can do this themselves. In the tenant portal, Rajesh opens My Payments. Above his payment history is the same bar. He ticks the two receipts he needs, and selects Download receipts. Or he picks a year, and the whole year downloads in one go. A tenant with more than one contract first chooses the contract, so a renewal on the same unit is never mixed with the old one.
