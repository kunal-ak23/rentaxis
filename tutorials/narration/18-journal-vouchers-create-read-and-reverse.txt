Most journals in Miftah write themselves: posting a contract, clearing a cheque, closing a month. The journal voucher is for everything else. As the Accountant, open Journal Voucher. Every entry in the books is listed here, manual and automatic, with its document type: TCO for a tenancy contract, PDR for a cheque received, CIL for month-end recognition, and JV for a manual journal. Filter on JV. In April, the cash from Arjun Mehta's returned cheque was banked. JV-26/1 debits the Emirates Islamic account 14,000 and credits the Cash Account 14,000. The money moved from the cash box to the bank, so no tenant balance changes. Now a correction. A 600 deep-cleaning bill was booked to Repairs and Maintenance. Select New Journal Voucher. Enter the date, 30 September, after the locked period; the property, Palm Ridge Residences; and a narration that tells the next reader why. Debit Cleaning Services 600. Until the other side is entered, Miftah shows the difference and will not post. Credit Repairs and Maintenance 600. Debits now equal credits, so Post is enabled. JV-26/3 is posted. Now suppose the bill had already been moved. You never edit or delete a posted journal. Select Reverse, keep 30 September, give a reason, and confirm. Miftah posts JV-26/4, the mirror image, linked as the reversal of JV-26/3. The original stays in the ledger, marked Reversed, and the two net to zero. Journals the system writes, such as a contract's TCO, have no Reverse button here. They are corrected from their own document, so the contract and its ledger never disagree.
