This tutorial sets up a partner business and a coupon offer for the Tenants of Oasis Crest Properties. As the Company Admin, open Promotions and select the Businesses tab, then Add business. Enter the partner's name in English and Arabic, choose its category, and add a phone and WhatsApp number. Allowed link domains limit where this business's ads may send a Tenant. Keep it Active and select Save. Now open the Ads tab and select Add ad. Choose the business, then give the ad a short title and an eyebrow line in English and Arabic, and a card colour. The preview on the right follows as you type. Under What happens on tap, an ad can open a website, show a coupon code, call the business or open WhatsApp. Choose Show coupon code, then enter the code and its terms in both languages. There is no separate expiry date: the ad's end date ends the offer. Set the start and end dates and the priority; a higher priority gets more airtime in the home carousel. Where it shows chooses between the home carousel and offers, or the offers screen only. Show to limits the ad to selected properties, here the Residence Tower, so other buildings never see it. Check the preview in Arabic, then select Save. The new ad is Live. Views and Taps count how often Tenants see and tap it in the mobile app, so a new ad starts at zero. Filter by business to review everything one partner is running. The ad stops showing on its own end date, and its views and taps stay on the row, so you can judge whether the offer was worth running.
