This tutorial creates a partner business and a targeted ad from the Company Admin side of Promotions. Open Promotions and start with Businesses. Search for the prepared partner before selecting Add business. Enter its synthetic English and Arabic names, category, contact details, and active status, then save. Switch to the Ads tab and select Add ad for that business. Add a concise English and Arabic title and eyebrow line, an approved image, a card colour, and the start and end dates. Choose what happens on tap — open a website, show a coupon code, call the business, or open WhatsApp. For a coupon, enter the code and terms; there is no separate expiry field, since the ad's own end date controls that. Choose where it shows — the home carousel and offers, or the offers screen only — and set which properties see it, so the offer is not shown to the wrong Tenants. Check the live preview in both languages, then save and confirm the ad is Live. Back on the Ads tab, the Views and Taps columns track engagement once Tenants start seeing the offer in the mobile app — a freshly created campaign starts at zero until then. Filter the Ads list by business to review everything running for one partner at a glance. When a promotion should stop showing, edit it and clear the active checkbox, or let it run out on its own end date; either way its history and totals stay on the row. A clear business record, a well-targeted ad, and an honest read of its engagement are what keep this offer worth a Tenant's attention.
