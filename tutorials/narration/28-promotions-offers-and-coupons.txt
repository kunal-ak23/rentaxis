Open Promotions and Offers in the tenant workspace. Review the participating businesses, campaign status labels, targeting scope, active dates, and the actions available to administrators before editing an offer.

Use the promotion editor to compare bilingual copy, placement, dates, property targeting, call-to-action type, coupon code, and terms. Keep the audience and redemption instructions precise so a resident sees the correct offer.

Finish on engagement analytics. Impressions, clicks, detail views, and coupon actions are aggregate campaign signals; refresh once when counts update and avoid exposing individual resident identity in marketing records.
