This tutorial covers cheque-failure fines, from settings to a Tenant's own view. Open Settings, then Rent and fines, inside Oasis Crest Properties. Review the approved fine amount for a bounced cheque, a signature mismatch and a closed account, along with the grace days and the per-day late rate. These are the only numbers that should ever change, and only with sign-off, because a rule in force today should not be read back onto an older penalty. Open Ahmed Hassan's tenancy contract and go to its Payments tab. A single bounced cheque does not cross the automatic threshold, so choose Raise Penalty and select the cheque-failure reason that actually applies. Confirm once. A raised penalty is proposed, not charged — it now sits in the Penalties queue waiting on Finance, and nothing has posted to the ledger yet. Open Cheque and Cash Collection, then the Penalties pill, and approve it. Only an approved penalty is a real charge; from here it can also be waived, with an authorisation note that records who approved the exception and why, so the original penalty stays on record either way. Switch to the Tenant's own view. Approved penalties, and only approved ones, appear on their Penalties page with the reason, amount and date attached. Back on the collection side, the fine is collected the same way as any other charge on the lease, as a cash receipt or through the next cheque, so it shows up wherever the rest of that Tenant's payments do. Configure the rule once, raise the exception with the right reason, approve before it is real, and the Tenant always sees exactly what they were charged and why.
