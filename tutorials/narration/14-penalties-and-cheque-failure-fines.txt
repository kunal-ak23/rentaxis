This tutorial covers penalties, fines and maintenance recharges: how they are proposed, how finance decides them, and the journal each decision writes. As the Company Admin, open Settings, then Rent and fines. Cheque-failure fines are set once for the organisation: 500 for a bounced cheque, 500 for a signature mismatch, and 1,000 for a closed account. A penalty is proposed automatically after 2 returned cheques on one contract; proposing one when rent clears late is a separate switch, off here. These rules only propose; nothing posts by itself. Now, as the Accountant, open Cheque and Cash Collection and choose Penalties. The Proposed tab holds two items. Mohammed Al Rashid's June instalment cleared after the grace period, so a late payment penalty of 250 was raised. Hana Yoshida's is a maintenance recharge of 800, raised from ticket TKT-26/1 for a cracked balcony glass panel. Next to Approve, Waive would drop a proposed item without posting anything, and Reduce would lower the amount. Approve Mohammed's penalty, dated today. Approval is the only step that posts. Approve Hana's recharge the same way. Now open Journal Voucher and filter to PEN. Mohammed's journal debits Rent Receivable and credits Rent Penalty with 250, so he now owes it, and it is collected like any other charge. Hana's debits Rent Receivable and credits Maintenance Charges with 800: the landlord paid for the repair, and the tenant pays it back. Back in Penalties, the Approved tab now lists three items, including Layla Nasser's 500 fine for her returned cheque. Each approved item offers Reverse. Reversing posts the mirror image of its journal, so a mistake is corrected in the books and nothing is deleted.
