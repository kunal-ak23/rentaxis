This tutorial creates, publishes, and withdraws a property listing in Oasis Crest Properties. Open Listings and select New Listing. Choose the prepared vacant property and unit — an occupied unit should not be marketed as vacant. Enter the title, English and Arabic descriptions, annual rent, security deposit, minimum contract term, and amenities on the Details and Pricing tabs, using only approved synthetic values with no internal notes. Move to Media and upload only approved demo images, then set the cover photo and confirm every photo belongs to this property. Check the SEO tab's search preview, and set the coordinates on Location. Select Publish once and confirm the listing moves to Published. Copy its public URL and open it in a separate session to verify the card and detail page match what was entered. Back in the editor, open View Enquiries. Interested Tenants appear here with their name, contact details, note, date, and status, ready for follow-up or to create a tenancy contract directly from an enquiry. When a unit becomes temporarily unavailable, select Unlist — it leaves the public marketplace while staying editable and keeping its enquiry history. Make the prepared edit, republish, and confirm the same accuracy check applies every time. Delete only an erroneous synthetic listing that should not remain in history and carries no audit value. Finish with the listing unlisted until the production recording fixture is approved.
