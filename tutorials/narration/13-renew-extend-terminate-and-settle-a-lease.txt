This tutorial reviews the three audited paths around a lease end without mutating the fresh tutorial tenant. Start in the administrator lease queue and compare active and pending-signature statuses, end dates, and available actions. Open the prepared active Ahmed Hassan lease to review current terms and the Extend Lease control; an extension changes the approved end date and should only be confirmed after reviewing any schedule impact. Do not submit an extension in this rehearsal. Switch to the prepared renter session and open Renewals. The renter view is working, but this fixture is beyond the 90-day notice window, so it truthfully reports that no renewal is currently due. In a live lease, the renewal banner and intent response appear only when the lease enters the configured window. Return to the administrator session and open the separate Rajesh Kumar lease. Select Terminate to enter the settlement preview, then review unpaid rent, open penalties, security deposit, existing charges, and the suggested refund or balance. A settlement preview is safe to inspect; do not add deductions, upload evidence, save a draft, or finalize the irreversible state in this recording. Renewal, extension, and termination remain distinct outcomes, and the selected action must match the real agreement and approval state.
