This tutorial moves from dashboard indicators to the available financial report types. Work as an authorised administrator in the Tutorial Studio organisation and keep the walkthrough read-only.

Start on the operational dashboard. The organisation switcher confirms the current tenant, while the KPI cards provide signals such as occupancy, collection, outstanding amounts, income, and expenses. A card is a starting point, not the complete accounting explanation. When a number looks unusual, open the underlying workflow and check its property and period scope before drawing a conclusion.

Open Financial Reports. The report screen lets you choose organisation or property scope, add start and end dates, and generate the selected report. Profit and Loss presents income, direct and indirect expenses, and net result. Balance Sheet is for assets, liabilities, and equity at a point in time. Trial Balance compares debit and credit totals. Aging groups outstanding receivables by age so collection work can be prioritised.

VAT Return is the period-based tax view and requires an appropriate date range. Ticket Reporting connects operational workload to the selected property and period. These tabs may be available even when a report has not yet been generated; the empty-state instruction is a reminder to choose scope and dates deliberately. Different reports answer different questions, so do not compare totals that use different filters.

Finish by selecting Profit and Loss at organisation scope and generating the report. The prepared organisation report shows total income, total expenses, net operating income, net profit, and the income and expense breakdowns beneath them. Use those totals as a reconciliation checkpoint against transactions, account mappings, and payment schedules. Export or share only through an approved channel after checking the file for sensitive information, and restore the same organisation and property scope before leaving the screen.
