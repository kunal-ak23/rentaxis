Every report in Miftah is read from the same ledger, so the reports always agree with each other. As the Accountant, open Profit and Loss for the year from 1 September 2025 to 31 August 2026. Rental income is 567,333.63: rent earned day by day, not cash collected. Fees, parking, maintenance charges and the cheque return penalty sit beside it. The 5,000 recovered from a written-off debt is other income, and the write-off itself, 11,326.03, is an expense. Net operating income for the year is 494,787.54, and the badge confirms that the report ties to the ledger. Open the Property Profit Report to see each building on its own. Pick August 2026 and compare it with the previous period. The Business Centre and the Residences each show their income, expenses and NOI, with the change against July beside them. Next, the Balance Sheet as at 31 August 2026. PDC receivable, 165,985, is the paper still in the drawer. Advance rent, 214,145.83, is rent billed but not yet earned, and the 42,200 of security deposits are held for tenants. Retained earnings carry the closed 2025 year, and this year's result sits below them. Assets equal liabilities plus equity, in every column. The Owner Statement gives one building's month in a form you can send to its owner: income and NOI, instalments due, what was collected and what is outstanding, deposits held and expenses, with a PDF in English or Arabic. Finally, aging. Payables aging lists what is owed to each supplier by days past due, as of any date, and ties every total to the supplier's ledger. For tenants, the aging strip on Cheque and Cash Collection sorts what is due by how many days it is overdue.
