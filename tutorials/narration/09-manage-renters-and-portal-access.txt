This tutorial covers renter profiles and resident portal access. Open Renters inside the RentAxis Tutorial Studio organization and review the prepared synthetic profiles. Select Add Renter to preview the bilingual name, contact, preferred-language, and portal-account fields; leave the form unsaved so the tutorial tenant stays deterministic. The portal-account checkbox explains that a renter profile stores contact information, while a portal account grants the ability to sign in. Never record a temporary credential or place a real identity document number in a tutorial tenant. Close the form and open a prepared active lease to show how the renter, unit, payment schedule, and tenancy status stay connected. Return to the renter directory to show where an authorised administrator starts portal-access work, while keeping credentials out of recordings. Use a prepared renter session for the resident-facing experience in the dedicated portal tutorial, where only renter navigation is available. Finish by confirming that the prepared renter can be selected in the lease wizard without creating duplicate resident records.
