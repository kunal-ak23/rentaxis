This tutorial takes cheques from the office drawer to the bank, and shows the one journal that banking writes. As the Accountant, open Cheque and Cash Collection. The Cheque register holds every cheque from every posted contract. Filter the status to Registered. Registered means the cheque is recorded and its post-dated cheque journal is written, but the paper is still in the office. Now open To deposit. It lists the registered cheques whose date has arrived. Mohammed Al Rashid's cheque 880102 for 19,500 matured on 1 September. Select it and choose Deposit Batch. Enter the deposit date and confirm. The cheque moves to Deposited. Banking writes no journal at all, because nothing has changed about what the tenant owes. Back in the register, find Hana Yoshida's cheque 770109 for 5,500. It is already Deposited, and today the bank has confirmed it, so choose Clear. The dialog shows the account it clears into, the Emirates Islamic account for Palm Ridge Residences. Confirm, and the cheque is Cleared. Clearing writes the receipt journal. Open Journal Voucher and filter to CRT: the newest one debits the Emirates Islamic account with 5,500, and credits PDC Receivable with 5,500. The money becomes the company's when the bank pays it, not when the cheque is handed over. Finally, look at the summary above the register. The tiles total what is registered, deposited and cleared this month, and the aging strip sorts what is due by how many days it is overdue. What is overdue comes from the register, cheque by cheque.
