Parking bays and amenities can now charge by the day or by the month, and every fee goes straight into the books. Open the property, then its Parking tab, and edit bay B2-18. Set the fee type to Per month, and the amount to five hundred dirhams. The income account is optional. Left empty, parking fees go to the property's Additional Parking account. Save. The office can also book for a tenant. In Bookings, select New booking. Choose the property and the unit, and the tenant on its live contract fills in by itself. Choose Parking, bay B2-18, from today for one month. The fee is worked out before you book: five hundred dirhams, plus VAT where it applies. Select Book and approve. The booking is approved at once, and the fee is posted as a journal voucher. Rent Receivable for Ahmed is debited five hundred, and Additional Parking income is credited five hundred. The narration names the bay and the dates. On Ahmed's contract, a row now collects the five hundred, like any other payment, and he sees it in his payments. Plans change. Ahmed hands the bay back the same day. Open the booking and select Release. Today counts as used; the rest of the month is credited. A second voucher takes four hundred and eighty-three dirhams, thirty-three back out of parking income, and the row Ahmed still has to pay drops to sixteen dirhams, sixty-seven. A booking that has not started yet is cancelled instead, and its whole fee is reversed. Tenants see the fee before they ask. In the tenant portal, Fatima opens Facilities. The Fitness Centre costs thirty dirhams a day. She picks a date, and the fee is shown before she submits: thirty dirhams. When the office approves, the fee is charged to her contract in the same way, and she is told the amount.
