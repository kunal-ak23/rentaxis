This tutorial reviews the financial ledger without adding or changing a transaction. Use an authorised tenant administrator and start in Finance, Transactions.

The transaction workspace separates money in from money out and keeps every row tied to a date, description, property, and, when relevant, a unit. The prepared list includes rental-income installments, a cheque-bounce entry, and split expense rows such as common-area cleaning and equipment repairs. The totals at the bottom make the current scope explicit. Add Transaction is available for approved manual entries, but a manual entry should never duplicate rent, cheque, penalty, settlement, or other events that RentAxis already posts automatically.

Open Filter to narrow the list. Property, account type, start date, and end date can be combined before selecting Apply Filters. Use a clear reference and description when a manual transaction is genuinely required so another operator can reconcile it to the approved source. Choose an expense account for an expense instead of entering a negative income value, and leave the unit empty when the cost belongs to the whole property. If an entry is wrong, use the approved reversal or correction process rather than silently changing its meaning.

Finish in Reports and generate the organisation-level Profit and Loss report. The report turns the journal into totals such as Total Income and Net Operating Income for the selected scope. Move from the individual transaction, through its filters, to the generated report when reconciling a period. Finance should compare the report with source documents and the account mappings before enabling live operations.
