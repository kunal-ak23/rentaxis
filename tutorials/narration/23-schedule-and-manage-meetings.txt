This tutorial reviews how RentAxis keeps meetings and property visits in one shared record. Use an authorised tenant administrator and keep the prepared appointment read-only.

The Meetings screen can switch between Calendar and List views. Status, type, and purpose filters narrow the queue, while month, week, and day controls change the calendar scope. The prepared calendar shows a replacement-cheque appointment and a property viewing in the current month. New Meeting is available when an appointment has passed the normal scheduling checks; use a clear purpose, the correct property and unit, the intended participant, and the local time zone.

Open the approved replacement-cheque meeting. Its detail confirms the title, purpose type, status, date, time window, duration, host, requester, linked lease, property, unit, cheque count, and notes. The status controls make the allowed lifecycle explicit: Complete after the appointment occurs, Mark No Show when the participant did not attend, or Cancel when plans change. Preserve a useful reason and the shared record instead of deleting the appointment. Any update should be submitted once and allowed to finish before another action is attempted.

Return to the calendar for follow-up. The same appointment remains in its expected time slot, and the filters make meetings needing approval or completion easy to find. Consistent status and time-zone handling keeps notifications, dashboards, and both participants’ views aligned.
