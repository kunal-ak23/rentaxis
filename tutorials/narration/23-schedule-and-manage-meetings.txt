This tutorial books a meeting in Oasis Crest Properties: a Tenant asks for one, the Company Admin approves it, and meetings that are over get closed off. Signed in as the Tenant Ahmed Hassan, open Meetings and select New Meeting. Choose Office Visit, then Next; a Property Visit would meet at the unit instead. The purpose is Tenancy Contract Renewal, for his contract on unit A-101. Pick a date, and the wizard lists the host's free slots; take the first one. For a renewal the Tenant can propose terms: twelve months at 88,000, with a short note. The last step lists every choice for a final check; select Request Meeting. It is listed as Requested, with the Company Admin as the host. Now sign in as the Company Admin. The calendar shows every meeting, and the new request sits on its day. Open it to see the date and time, who asked, the linked contract and the renewal proposal. Select Approve. The meeting is now Approved, with Complete, Mark No Show and Cancel available. Meetings in the past need closing off. Fatima Al Zaabi's cheque replacement visit took place on the twenty-ninth of September, so open it and select Complete. The penthouse viewing on the thirtieth was never confirmed and did not take place. Select Cancel, so it stays on record as Cancelled instead of being deleted. Finally, filter the list by status: Approved shows the meetings still to come.
