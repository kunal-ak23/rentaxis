This tutorial schedules a meeting and keeps its status visible to both the property team and the Tenant. Sign in with the prepared Tenant or manager account, open Meetings, and select New Meeting. The wizard walks through five steps: Type, where you choose an office visit or a property visit; Context, where you pick the purpose — cheque replacement, tenancy contract renewal, a property viewing, or another reason — and the property, unit or contract it concerns; Date and Slot, for the actual appointment time; Details, for notes or anything specific to the purpose; and Review, before submitting. If the host cannot be worked out automatically, the wizard also asks which property manager should hold the meeting. Submit once and open the meeting detail. Confirm the purpose, host, requester, property, unit, time and initial status. Switch to the other account and open Meetings — the same appointment should be visible with the information appropriate to that role. Approve or confirm the meeting when that step is required, then open the calendar or filtered list and verify it sits in the expected slot. When plans change, set the status to Cancelled with a useful reason instead of deleting the record. Once a meeting has actually taken place, mark it Complete so it leaves the active queue while staying in history. Accurate status keeps the calendar and notifications reliable and gives both parties one shared record of the appointment.
