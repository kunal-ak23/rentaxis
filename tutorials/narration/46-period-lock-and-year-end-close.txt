This tutorial closes periods and a fiscal year in Miftah. Open Accounting and choose Year End Closing. The period lock reads 30 June 2026. No journal can be dated on or before that day, from any screen, so a month you have reported cannot change underneath you. September has ended, so enter 30 September 2026 and select Lock through. Miftah refuses. September's rent has not been recognised yet, and a lock now would strand that income in a closed period. The message names the month and how many periods are still planned. Select the link to open Month-end recognition. Set the date to 30 September and preview. Nine contract periods for September are still planned. Run recognition and confirm. Each period becomes one income journal. It moves that month's rent from Advance Rent to Rental Income, at the contract's day rate for every day of September. A run never posts the same period twice, so it is safe to repeat. When the run finishes, Miftah shows nine posted, and the warning at the top is gone. Back on Year End Closing, lock through 30 September again. This time the lock moves, and the sidebar shows the new date. September is posted, so nothing is stranded. Now read the Fiscal years list. The year 2025 is Closed. Palm Ridge's books started on 1 September 2025, so that year held four months. To open its year-end closing journal, go to Journal Voucher, set the type to YEC, apply, and open YEC-25/1. It empties every income account and every expense account for the year, building by building, and puts the net result into Retained Earnings, the equity account that carries the profit the business has kept. Now open the Trial Balance, enter 31 December 2025 as the date, and select Apply. Each income and expense account shows equal debits and credits, so its balance is zero, and Retained Earnings holds the year's profit. Select Before closing entries to see the same day as the accountant reviewed it before the close. Now income shows only its credit for the year, and the expenses only their debit. Back on Year End Closing, open the close preview for 2026. The year has not ended, so Miftah lists what still stands in the way: the year end must be in the past, the rest of the year's income must be recognised, and VAT tax points must be declared. Below, the preview shows the net result each building will carry into Retained Earnings. If a closed year must change, the Company Admin selects Re-open and gives a reason. Miftah reverses the closing journal and moves the lock back to the start of that year. Post the correction, then close the year again. The first closing journal stays, marked as reversed, so the trail is complete.
