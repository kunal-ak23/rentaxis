This tutorial closes periods and a fiscal year in Miftah. Open Accounting and choose Year End Closing. The period lock reads 30 June 2026. No journal can be dated on or before that day, from any screen, so a quarter you have reported cannot change underneath you. To move the lock forward, enter the new date. Miftah refuses if monthly income or VAT tax points up to that date have not been posted yet, because the lock would strand them. Now read the Fiscal years list. The year 2025 is Closed. Palm Ridge's books started on 1 September 2025, so that year held four months. Open its year-end closing journal. It empties every income account and every expense account for the year, building by building, and puts the net result into Retained Earnings. Retained Earnings is the equity account that carries the profit the business has kept from earlier years. Now open the Trial Balance as of 31 December 2025. Income and expense are zero and Retained Earnings holds the year's profit. Select Before closing entries to see the same day as the accountant reviewed it before the close. A new year's Profit and Loss therefore starts from zero, while the balance sheet carries the profit forward. Back on Year End Closing, open the close preview for 2026. The year has not ended, so Miftah lists what still stands in the way: the year end must be in the past, the previous year must be closed, and income and VAT must be posted to the year end. Draft vouchers are a warning you can override. If a closed year must change, the Company Admin selects Re-open and gives a reason. Miftah reverses the closing journal and moves the lock back to the start of that year, which also unlocks every later period. Post the correction, then close the year again. The first closing journal stays, marked as reversed, so the trail is complete.
