## English (en-GB)

### Short description

Run properties, leases, payments, listings and gate operations in one place.

### Full description

Property operations, in control.

Miftah Manager gives authorised property teams a clear, mobile view of day-to-day operations—from occupancy and collections to maintenance, listings and visitor access.

With Miftah Manager you can:

• Monitor occupancy, collections, overdue amounts and operational priorities
• Manage properties, units, leases, renewals and resident records
• Track cheques and payments, scan cheque details and download receipts
• Review maintenance tickets, meetings, staff and vendors
• Publish rental listings and respond to active interests
• Review lease settlements, penalties and supporting documents
• Manage visitor-pass approvals, guards, access policies and gate vendors
• Work with finance accounts, transactions, reports and property mappings
• Switch between English and Arabic, light and dark appearance

Miftah Manager requires an authorised account issued by a participating property organisation. Access and available features follow your assigned role and the organisation’s configuration. Payment and cheque tools manage the property organisation’s authorised transaction records.

### Release notes

The first Google Play release of Miftah Manager brings portfolio dashboards, property and lease operations, cheque and payment workflows, maintenance, listings, finance, reports and gate-access administration to authorised property teams.
