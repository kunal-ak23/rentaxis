# Play Console submission checklist

## Per app

- Create the app with the exact package/app name and default language in its listing file.
- Enrol in Play App Signing and upload the app-specific, upload-key-signed `.aab`.
- Complete the English and Arabic store listings and upload the 512 px icon, 1024 × 500 feature graphic and phone screenshots.
- Set app access to restricted and provide a durable reviewer account/instructions.
- Complete Ads, Content rating, Target audience, News, Government, Data safety and Financial features declarations.
- Set the privacy-policy and data-deletion URLs after the web pages are deployed and verified publicly.
- Choose countries/regions, pricing (Free) and the production rollout.

## Reviewer access

- Renter: use a dedicated resident demo account with stable seeded data. Do not publish credentials in the public listing.
- Manager: use a dedicated tenant-admin demo account with stable seeded data and no access to real customer tenants.
- Security: configure a Firebase test phone number and fixed verification code, connect it to a dedicated guard in the demo tenant, and supply both only in Play’s App access instructions. Reviewers must be able to reach the board and validate a reusable pass without receiving a real SMS.
